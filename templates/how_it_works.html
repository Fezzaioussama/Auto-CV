How Auto-CV moves from offer text to an application-ready CV.

Process

Four checkpoints from input to output

The page mirrors the app flow so each visual maps to one action you take in the workspace.

Step 01

Paste or upload the job offer

Auto-CV reads the offer and separates the role into practical signals: required skills, qualifications, responsibilities, and keywords that matter for ATS matching.

  • Supports pasted text and uploaded job files.
  • Keeps the original offer available for review.
  • Creates the skill list used by the next step.
Step 02

Add your CV

Upload a LaTeX CV, PDF, Word document, image scan, or paste the source directly. The app prepares the content for comparison without changing your facts.

  • Reads multiple CV formats.
  • Preserves editable LaTeX when available.
  • Structures your profile for precise matching.
Step 03

Compare, rewrite, and review

The optimizer shows matched skills, missing signals, ATS priorities, and section rewrites. You approve, reject, or edit each suggested change before it affects the final CV.

  • Match score and skill coverage show the gap.
  • Rewrites stay grounded in your existing CV.
  • Optional additions are marked for your input.
Step 04

Export, save, and prepare

Render the PDF, copy or download the corrected LaTeX, save the tailored CV with the job, then reuse the same context to generate interview questions.

  • PDF rendering keeps a preview in the workspace.
  • Saved jobs and CV versions are reusable.
  • Interview prep starts from the same CV and offer.
Ready

Start with the offer, then keep every change under review.

The workflow is designed for fast tailoring without turning the CV into unverified claims.
