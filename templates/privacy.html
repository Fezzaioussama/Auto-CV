What we collect

  • Account data: your email address, optional name, and a hashed password.
  • Content you provide: CVs you upload or paste, job descriptions, and the tailored CVs, cover letters and analyses we generate. CVs typically contain personal data (name, contact details, work history).
  • Technical data: standard server logs (IP address, timestamps) used for security, rate limiting and debugging.

How we use it

We process your content solely to provide the service: parsing job offers, analysing and rewriting your CV, generating cover letters and interview questions, and storing your saved workspace so you can return to it.

AI processing & sub-processors

To generate and analyse content, the text of your CV and job descriptions may be sent to a third-party Large Language Model provider ([e.g. OpenRouter / your self-hosted model]) acting as our processor. We send only what is needed to perform the request. Review your provider's data handling and retention terms and reflect them here.

Retention

We keep your account and saved content until you delete it or close your account. You can delete individual jobs/CVs at any time, or delete your entire account from the Account page.

Your rights

If you are in the EU/EEA (GDPR) or similar jurisdictions, you have the right to access, correct, export and erase your data, and to object to or restrict processing. You can:

  • Access & export: download all your data as JSON from the Account page.
  • Erase: permanently delete your account and data from the same page.

To exercise any other right, contact [privacy@your-domain]. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Security

Passwords are stored hashed. Connections use HTTPS in production. No method is perfectly secure, but we apply reasonable safeguards.
